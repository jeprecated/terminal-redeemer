## Goal

Let Overton continuously project one selected Lattice Niri workspace or all eligible Lattice terminal sources, selectively close/reopen individual projections, and manage the live slice through a controller-backed TUI and keyboard-ready direct argv while preserving host ownership, exact attachment, bounded recovery, and disabled-by-default rollout.
